SWAPNIL 3D PORTFOLIO
======================

PREVIEW
1. Extract this ZIP.
2. Open index.html in Google Chrome.
3. Use the navigation to explore all pages.

The site is zero-build: no npm, Node.js or React setup is required.
Internet is required for Google Fonts, Font Awesome and the Three.js CDN.

PAGES
- index.html
- about.html
- experience.html
- projects.html
- services.html
- pricing.html
- contact.html

CUSTOMIZE
- Replace the profile placeholder in the HTML with your own image.
- Replace # social links with your real LinkedIn/GitHub/Instagram URLs.
- Replace project placeholder art with screenshots.
- Edit pricing in pricing.html.
- Connect the contact form to Formspree, Netlify Forms or your WordPress backend before launch.

NOTE
Project information and professional details are based on the supplied resume. Project URLs included are the live-project URLs listed in the resume.
